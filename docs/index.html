When the Simulator Fails,
Not the Policy

Many failures in simulated robot benchmarks are the simulator's fault, not the policy's. We find the cause and fix it.

01 · The problem

The policy places the can.
The simulator throws it out.

LingBot-VA puts a 10 g can into the basket. No robot link touches it, and the fastest link moves at under 0.1 m/s. Then, within one 4 ms physics step, the can speeds up from 0.2 to 22 m/s. The benchmark records a failure.

02 · SimuGuard

An auditing plugin.
The evaluation stays the same.

SimuGuard attaches to the benchmark's own evaluation script on LIBERO, RoboTwin, RoboCasa and RoboDojo. It records every physics step, flags objects that move faster than the robot or gravity can explain, replays any episode bit for bit, and reruns it with one change.

01 · RECORD

Every step

State, controls and contacts at every physics step, with no change to the evaluation.

02 · DETECT

Three stages

A contact that starts motion, then a gravity check, then a carrier check. 37 of 40 flagged clips judged implausible by five engineers.

03 · REPLAY

Bit for bit

Every recorded episode reproduces exactly, so a failure can be studied step by step.

04 · RERUN

One change

Same seed, same actions, one simulation parameter changed, scored by the benchmark's own success check.

03 · The cause

One constant.

The two convex pieces that start the ejection are 21 mm apart. PhysX 5.3.1 reports them 27.5 mm deep, with the normal pointing the wrong way, and the solver removes that depth in one step.

Its GJK distance algorithm discards a triangle of its simplex when the area test below is true. The threshold is fixed in meters. RoboTwin's containers are cut into pieces one to two centimeters across, so valid triangles fall under it.

06 · Four benchmarks, two engines

Not only RoboTwin.

On every benchmark we tested, an object at rest can be thrown in a single physics step, though nothing pushes it that hard. On RoboTwin we traced it to the GJK threshold. RoboDojo runs Isaac Sim with contact generation on the GPU and also shows a false contact, on a path we have not located. On MuJoCo, these events are few and slow.

07 · What to report

A success rate in simulation counts the simulator's failures too.

Benchmarks on PhysX 5.3.1 or earlier should rerun in a repaired engine. Where the engine cannot change, report the success rate together with the range it spans when every flagged episode fails or passes.

LingBot-VA, five container tasks: reported 83.1%, replay estimate 91.9%, excluding flagged episodes 98.0%.
